## What this means

- **Same verdict as crypto.** The strategies assigned to ETFs in
  `asset_universe.yaml` (SMA crossover) have **no demonstrated edge** on real ETF
  data. They had never been tested on it before this run; now they have.
- **Equities are an even higher bar than BTC.** SPY/QQQ/SOXX had massive secular
  uptrends (SOXX +4360% over 25 years). Buy-and-hold captures all of it with one
  entry; any tactical strategy that steps aside gives up compounding. Unlike some
  crypto strategies, these all made *positive* money — just far less than holding,
  and at a lower Sharpe.
- **The gap has never been tests — it's edge.** The Alpaca adapter and ETF
  backfill are implemented and unit-tested (green). ETFs stay `status: research`;
  no paper/live promotion until a strategy clears this same OOS bar.

## Methodology

- **Rolling walk-forward.** Train ≈ 2 years (504 daily bars), test ≈ 6 months
  (126 bars), rolled across the full history (45–62 windows per ETF).
- **In-sample tuning only.** Parameters grid-searched for best in-sample Sharpe
  (≥3–5 trade floor) on each train window, applied only to the next unseen test
  window.
- **Honest benchmark.** Buy-and-hold (dividend-adjusted) over the identical OOS
  span, one entry fee. Daily annualisation (252 trading days/year).
