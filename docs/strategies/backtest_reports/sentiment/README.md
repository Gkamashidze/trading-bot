# Sentiment / Positioning Walk-Forward Reports (BTC)

Non-price signal edge test. After every price-based strategy failed to beat
buy-and-hold (see `../README.md` and `../etf/README.md`), this asks whether
**crowd sentiment** (Fear & Greed) or **derivatives positioning** (funding rate)
contain edge that price does not. Generated by
`scripts/run_sentiment_walk_forward.py` on daily BTC 2018–2026 with merged
signals (`scripts/download_signal_data.py`).

## Bottom line (2026-07-05) — the first risk-adjusted edge found

After ~20 price-based strategy/asset tests with zero winners, a **contrarian
entry + trend-following exit** hybrid is the first strategy to **beat
buy-and-hold on a risk-adjusted basis** out-of-sample:

| Strategy | OOS return | Sharpe | Max DD | Trades | Beats B&H? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Buy & hold BTC** | +638.5% | **0.77** | −76.6% | 1 | — |
| Fear&Greed contrarian | −67.0% | −0.12 | −73.6% | 10 | ❌ |
| Funding contrarian | +222.7% | 0.58 | −74.5% | 15 | ❌ |
| **Sentiment→trend hybrid** | **+592.5%** | **0.89** ✅ | **−56.2%** ✅ | 58 | risk-adj ✅ |

The hybrid delivers ~93% of buy-and-hold's return with **a higher Sharpe (0.89
vs 0.77) and a much smaller drawdown (−56% vs −77%)**. It does *not* beat raw
total return — `beats_benchmark` (return-only) reads NO — but on the metric that
actually matters for a leveraged or risk-budgeted allocation, it wins.

**Robustness:** re-run across 6 fill-model seeds, the hybrid's Sharpe stays
**0.82–0.93 (always > 0.77)** and max DD **−56% to −57.5% (always better than
−77%)**. The edge is not a single-seed artifact.

## Why it works (and the two that didn't)

- **Fear&Greed contrarian (−67%)** and **funding contrarian (+223%)** both use a
  contrarian *exit* — "sell into greed / high funding". That dumps the position
  early in every bull run and forfeits the parabolic upside. Selling strength is
  the wrong exit for a trending asset.
- **The hybrid keeps the good half and fixes the bad half:** contrarian *entry*
  (buy when funding is negative = crowded shorts, while price is above its MA),
  but a *trend* exit (leave only when price breaks below the MA). "Buy the dip,
  ride the trend." That is what converts a positioning signal into a positive
  risk-adjusted result: 24% win rate but the winners are large (trend-following
  profile), and the trend exit caps drawdowns at −56%.

## Multi-asset validation (2026-07-05) — real but asset-dependent

One asset, one split is not proof. `scripts/validate_sentiment_hybrid.py` reran
the hybrid on **BTC and ETH** across **three train/test window sizes**:

| Asset | Split | Strat Sharpe | B&H Sharpe | Max DD (strat vs B&H) | Edge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BTC | 504/126 | 0.89 | 0.77 | −56% vs −77% | Sharpe + DD ✅ |
| BTC | 365/90 | 1.03 | 0.90 | −50% vs −77% | Sharpe + DD ✅ |
| BTC | 730/180 | 1.16 | 0.84 | −48% vs −77% | Sharpe + DD ✅ |
| ETH | 504/126 | 0.75 | 0.73 | −67% vs −79% | Sharpe + DD ✅ |
| ETH | 365/90 | 0.31 | 0.81 | −79% vs −79% | DD only ⚠️ |
| ETH | 730/180 | 0.74 | 0.88 | −62% vs −79% | DD only ⚠️ |

**Verdict: real but not universal.** Sharpe beats buy-and-hold in **4 of 6**
combos. Two robust properties: it beats Sharpe on **BTC across all three splits**,
and it **reduces max drawdown in all six** combos (both assets, every split).
But on **ETH the Sharpe edge is marginal-to-absent** — the 365/90 split is a bad
miss (+29% vs +1030%; short windows whipsawed out of ETH's 2020–21 run). So the
hybrid is best read as a **drawdown-reducing overlay with a Sharpe edge that is
strong on BTC and unreliable on ETH** — not yet a universal alpha.

## Honest caveats

- **Beats on Sharpe + drawdown, not on raw return.** To turn the higher Sharpe
  into higher *return* you would size up (leverage) to match buy-and-hold's risk
  — which introduces its own execution and liquidation risks.
- **58 OOS trades over 7 years** — a real but not huge sample; a few big trend
  moves drive most of the result.
- **BTC only, one regime era.** Funding history starts 2019-09 (earlier windows
  fall back to Fear&Greed). Must be validated on other assets/periods before any
  promotion.
- Still `research` — no paper/live. `live_trading_enabled = false`.

## Methodology

Rolling walk-forward: train ≈ 2 years (504 daily bars), test ≈ 6 months (126),
20 windows over 2019–2026. Parameters tuned only on each train window by
in-sample Sharpe (≥3 trades), applied unseen to the next test window. Buy-and-hold
benchmark over the identical OOS span. 365-day annualisation. Realistic fees +
slippage.
